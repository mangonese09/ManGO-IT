**Date:** 2026-07-27 · **App version reviewed:** v0.10.0 live at `it.mangonese.dev` · **Repo state:** commit `4c0f96e`
**Method:** full repo read; 8 live Playwright journeys + degraded/offline sessions (assets in `docs/review-assets/`); 4 parallel web-research sweeps (Tier A/B patterns, rail+urban fares, coach fares); empirical probes of the Transitous/MOTIS API; local analysis of the shipped coach feed. ## 1. What this app currently is (Phase 0)

*(Reviewed with owner 2026-07-27; correction folded in: the Saved tab's populated states are covered in §6/§7, not just its empty state.)*

**A single-view Sicily A→B transit planner** — four tabs (Home, Saved, Map, Settings), mobile-first PWA, English UI with Italian place names. The core loop lives on one screen: an A→B search box above a geolocated nearby-departures board.

**Search input:** two stacked endpoint fields with debounced suggestions (Transitous geocode, Sicily-bbox-filtered, own coach stops merged in pre-ingestion), each row labeled by kind ("train station / coach stop / town / address"). Destination-first flow (picking a To with no From auto-geolocates and searches). One optional datetime behind a "Now" chip fronting a native picker, a Depart/Arrive-by toggle, Trains vs Buses+coaches filter pills, recents chips, star-to-save stops from suggestions.

**Results:** up to 6 itineraries from Transitous/MOTIS (proxy-tuned: 6 h searchWindow, 600 m matching, transfer cushion — `server/proxy.js:554-557`). Cards show dep→arr clocks dominant, duration, a proportional leg strip, transfer count, a worst-transfer risk chip (calm/tight/risky), and an honest live/scheduled badge. Detail sheets show per-leg stop lists, ViaggiaTreno live delay for Trenitalia legs, and a per-operator "🎫 Tickets" block — static how-to-buy text + outbound link, **no prices anywhere in the app**.

**The differentiator:** every search races the proxy's own-feed `/api/direct` (direct coaches + one-transfer chains computed from the app's own PDF→GTFS feed of ~40 Sicilian coach operators; 2,850 stops / 6,023 trips in `server/coach-*.json`). It renders three ways, all verified live `[observed]`: sole answer when the plan is empty (Raffadali→Catania), "Faster by coach" when it beats the plan by ≥15 min (Palermo→Agrigento: SAIS 09:15→11:15 vs a 3 h 23 m rail-replacement bus), and "Routing unavailable — our coaches only" when Transitous is dead (verified by aborting `/api/plan` at the network layer). Dead ends are never bare: next-day probe, nearest-served hint, feed-horizon note ("verified through 2026-09-17").

**Nearby board:** line-first rows ranked by soonest departure, two directions per line, 60 s refresh, pin-to-save. **Saved:** favorite stops with full-day schedule sheets (own-feed coach timetables included) + pinned departures re-matched to live data. **Map:** lazy self-hosted Leaflet + Carto tiles, stops loaded per viewport, markers open the same schedule sheet `[observed]`. **Settings:** theme, per-source freshness, update check, cache clear, strike warning, attributions.

**House style is data honesty:** "scheduled" badges wherever realtime is absent, staleness chips on every data surface, every GET cached in localStorage and served stale-with-timestamp on failure; offline reload serves the full shell from SW cache with an "offline — data from…" chip `[observed]`.

**What it is not (today):** no fares or prices; no whole-day or "later departures" results (one instant, up to 6 itineraries — though the direct-coach block does render a whole day); no booking (hard non-goal); no ferries; no push alerts. A Capacitor wrap exists (server-URL mode, `capacitor.config.json`) with the APK one version behind the web — irrelevant in server-URL mode.

## 2. Executive summary — the ten things that matter

1. **Fares are the biggest missing feature, and they are far more obtainable than the app assumes.** SAIS Trasporti per-OD fares are *already sitting in this repo* — the harvested booking API rows in `pipeline/data/saist/runs-*.jsonl` carry a `costo` field (`{"dep":"06:45","arr":"08:00","costo":"4.9"}` `[observed]`). Trenitalia regional fares are exactly computable from the official km-band table (verified against Trenitalia's own booking API on three Sicily routes `[observed]`). AMAT Palermo ships real GTFS `fare_attributes.txt` `[observed]`. AST and Cuffaro publish full tariff tables `[observed]`. §4 designs the feature; roughly 70% of legs the app renders can show a *sourced* price on day one.

2. **The departure-time model is the single largest UX gap, and the routing layer can serve the fix.** Today a search answers "around now (or around one picked instant)" with ≤6 itineraries and **no way to see later departures**. Empirical probes `[observed]`: an 18 h `searchWindow` returns the full service day (19 itineraries Palermo→Cefalù) in 2.3 s upstream / ~4 s cold through the proxy, 4.6 KB gzipped after slimming; `pageCursor` paging works (URL-encode the `LATER|…` cursor). §5 specifies the whole-day default.

3. **Blocker-grade interaction defect on the time control:** once a date is picked, tapping "Depart" (or anywhere in the when-row) silently **wipes the chosen date** — the ✕ clear button's expanded invisible hitbox (`css/styles.css:502` `.field-clear::after{inset:-6px}`) resolves against `.when-row{position:relative}` (`:546`) because `.when-clear` is `position:static` (`:541`), so it covers the entire row. Reproduced with a real tap: chip reverted "Wed 29 Jul · 09:00" → "Now" with zero feedback `[observed]`. A user who sets a date then switches to "Arrive by" searches the wrong day.

4. **Rate limiting mislabels itself and takes the lifeboat down with the ship.** A real 429 storm (120 requests; 66×429 `[observed]`) makes the app say "*the routing service may be unreachable*" — wrong diagnosis, no retry guidance — and the coaches-only fallback dies too because `/api/direct` shares the same bucket. `js/api.js:10-24` treats every non-OK identically.

5. **Silent failures exist exactly where the PRD forbids them.** Autocomplete death is invisible: with `/api/geocode` failing, typing produces nothing — no toast, no hint (`js/search.js:258-260` swallows the catch) `[observed]`. A traveler on dying rural data would blame their spelling.

6. **Pipeline residue leaks into the user's face.** Autocomplete offers "per C.da Marchesa) Sciacca (deviazione per C.da Marchesa)" `[observed screenshot]`; the feed contains a stop literally named "scolastico", 8 stops served by zero trips, 31 unbalanced-paren names, and 14 coordinate pileups of ≥6 stops on one point (worst: 14 stops at the Agrigento centroid) — all measured from `server/coach-stops.json`/`coach-trips.json` `[observed]`. Favoritable stops can answer "No departures today" with no reason (summer-suppressed *scolastico* service) — honest but unexplained.

7. **The ticket blocks are the right skeleton at the wrong depth.** Static two-line copy per operator (`js/operators.js`) with no fares, no channel specifics, and some claims the fare research contradicts in detail (e.g., AST tickets are valid 30 days but the A/R return leg expires 7 days after outbound validation; onboard purchase is "exceptional", not the norm `[observed]` at astsicilia.it). §4 replaces this with a fare-state model + one "how to buy" design borrowing Rome2Rio's three-tier handoff.

8. **Visual craft has improved but the type scale is drifting again.** Live census of the results screen: **12 distinct font sizes and 3 weights** (including compounded fractions 16.8/13.6/12.48/11.52 px from em-nesting, e.g. `.suggest-area{font-size:0.8em}` `css/styles.css:69`) vs Kayak's measured **6 sizes / 2 weights** on a denser screen `[observed]`. The QA-09 rem-scale rule has partially regressed.

9. **Trenitalia rail-replacement buses render as six anonymous, identical "BUS" cards** on Palermo→Agrigento `[observed screenshot]` — no route identity, no "rail replacement" label, and the ViaggiaTreno live lookup can never resolve them. The best coach alternative sits *below* six lookalike cards.

10. **The competitive posture is right and should stay.** The 2026-07-26 teardown's steals (dead-end fallbacks, clock-led cards, risk chips, line-first board) all shipped and survive re-inspection. What remains from Tier A is the time model (§5) and small honesty upgrades; from Tier B, Rome2Rio's fare-range + handoff patterns (§4). Kayak contributes craft discipline only — its booking-funnel IA is explicitly not imported (§3 Tier C).

### 4.2 Where fares should live: a proxy-served fare table — not GTFS-Fares

**Recommendation: a versioned `server/fares.json` consumed by the proxy, NOT GTFS-Fares v1/v2 emitted by the pipeline.** This contradicts the "keep everything in the GTFS" instinct, deliberately:

1. **Coverage mismatch.** GTFS-Fares in our feed could only ever describe *our coach feed's* legs. The legs that render most — Trenitalia, AMAT, AMTS, FCE — come from *other* feeds ingested by Transitous; our fares.txt would never attach to them. A proxy-side table covers every leg the app can name an operator for (`js/operators.js` matching already does this).
2. **Runtime mismatch.** MOTIS has only an *experimental* `withFares` plan option (`openapi.yaml`: "Experimental. If set to true, the response will contain fare information" `[observed]`), Transitous ingestion of our feed is still pending (PR #2327), and the client already gets ticket info by operator matching, not from routing responses. GTFS-Fares would be data at rest with no consumer.
3. **Model mismatch.** Half the matrix isn't expressible as exact GTFS fares: ranges (Interbus), dynamic (FlixBus), counter-only (Lumia), "band needs distance" (AST). The uncertainty model (4.3) *is* the feature; GTFS-Fares v2 has no "honestly unknown" state.
4. **Build-gate coupling.** Fares go stale on a different clock than schedules. Wiring them into `emit_gtfs.py`/`validate.py` means a fare-source outage blocks a schedule ship. Keep the gates separate: a small `validate_fares` check (schema + as-of dates + source URLs present) that warns, never blocks the feed.

Shape (proxy loads it next to `coach-stops.json`; served inside existing responses via a new `fares` lookup keyed by operator + optional OD):

```json
{ "operator": "SAIS Trasporti", "kind": "od-exact", "asOf": "2026-07-27",
  "source": "laser-orchard-harvest", "pairs": { "AG|CT": 13.10, "AG|CL": 6.30 } }
{ "operator": "AMAT Palermo", "kind": "flat", "asOf": "2026-07-27",
  "source": "https://opendata.comune.palermo.it/...", "single": 1.40, "onboard": 1.80,
  "passes": [{ "name": "Giornaliero", "price": 3.50, "beatsSinglesAt": 3 }] }
{ "operator": "Autolinee Lumia", "kind": "counter-only", "asOf": "2026-07-27",
  "note": "pay the driver — bring coins" }
```

The SAIS Trasporti pairs are generated by ~40 lines added to the *existing* `saist_harvest.py --build` step (the `costo` field is already in its cache); Trenitalia is a band function of route-km (see below); the rest is a hand-maintained table with as-of dates — exactly like `js/operators.js` today, but structured.

### 4.3 Uncertainty model — fare states

Never display a number the pipeline can't stand behind. Five states, each with fixed UI treatment (grammar from Rome2Rio B2 `[observed]`):

| State | When | UI treatment (390 px) |
|---|---|---|
| **exact** | Sourced flat/OD/band-resolved price, `asOf` ≤ 12 months | `€13.10` — plain, tabular-nums, no qualifier |
| **range** | Two bounding sources, or band with estimated distance | `€4.60–5.10 · estimated` — en-dash, muted "estimated" suffix |
| **zone/pass** | Urban flats where a pass may apply | `€1.40 · 90 min` + pass hint line (4.5) |
| **counter-only** | No published price exists | `Price at ticket office / from driver` — text, never a number |
| **stale** | `asOf` > 12 months or source unreachable at refresh | Price shown struck-muted with `verify — last checked Jul 2026` |

Staleness mechanics: each entry's `asOf` is stamped by whatever produced it (harvest run, manual edit). The existing weekly/monthly refresh crons re-stamp SAIS; a fare whose source 404s at refresh keeps its last value but flips to **stale**. `/api/health` grows a `fares: {oldestAsOf}` field so Settings' freshness panel can show it — same pattern as `feedHorizon` (`server/proxy.js:291-325`).

**AST distance estimation (flagged):** AST is band-priced; route-km can be approximated by summing haversine along the trip's stop chain (data already in `coach-trips.json`). Roads are longer than geodesics, so the estimate straddles at most two adjacent bands → display as a **range** spanning them ("€4.10–5.00 · estimated"), never as exact. Whether this is acceptable is Open Question #3.

### 4.4 Purchase channel per leg — the channel model

Per operator, ordered by what the traveler should actually do (all claims sourced in the matrix/§11):

- **Trenitalia:** app/site (auto-validating digital ticket) > station machine > tabaccheria. **Gotchas that cost money:** onboard purchase is *banned* boarding at Palermo C., Catania C., Messina C., Siracusa (fine risk €50–200 scale `[observed]` CGT); paper tickets validate before boarding (4 h validity); ~50 no-sales-network stations allow onboard purchase free *if you tell staff at boarding*. Rail-replacement buses use the same ticket.
- **AST:** site/app registration or **onboard from the driver (cash or card)** — but framed by AST as exceptional; tickets are undated (30-day validity), **must be stamped in the onboard machine**, and the A/R return leg dies 7 days after the outbound stamp `[observed]`.
- **SAIS (both):** online/booking portal, terminal biglietterie, e-ticket accepted. PA–CT trunk: buy before boarding.
- **Interbus/Etna/Segesta:** online/app; "buy before you reach the stop" is their own instruction `[observed]`; onboard sale is driver-discretionary folklore `[reported, low]` — never promise it.
- **Cuffaro:** online portal, offices, onboard **only at some stops** `[observed]`.
- **Lumia/SAL/Gallo/Camilleri:** pay the driver / counter; exact change culture (Lumia says bring coins) `[observed]`.
- **Prestia:** online/desk/onboard (+€0.30–1.00 onboard) `[reported]`.
- **AMAT/AMTS/TUA urban:** tabaccheria or app *before boarding*; validate every boarding including transfers; onboard costs more where offered at all (AMAT: driver, front door, exact change; no sales on the tram) `[observed]`. Fines €52–84.
- **FCE:** eticketing/app/counters; MetroBus integrated tickets when the itinerary mixes FCE metro + AMTS.
- **FlixBus:** app/site only; booking mandatory.

### 4.5 Pass detection — deliberately minimal

**Recommendation: rule-based day-pass hints for urban operators only; no fare optimization engine.** The data supports exactly one honest rule: flat-fare urban day passes beat singles at a known ride count (AMAT €3.50/€1.40 → 3 rides; AMTS €3.50/€1.40 → 3; TUA €3.40/€1.20 → 3; FCE 24h is ride-capped at 4 — hint only at 3+ metro rides). The itinerary sheet counts same-operator urban legs *in that itinerary* and, when ≥2, appends one line: *"Round trip or 3+ rides today? The €3.50 day pass beats singles."* No cross-operator arithmetic, no A/R optimization (AST's 7-day expiry and Interbus's same-day rule make automated A/R advice fine-print-dependent — the how-to-buy sheet states the discounts as text instead). Intercity day passes verified not to exist `[observed]` — nothing to detect.

## 5. Departure time model (Phase 3)

### 5.1 What the app does today (code-cited)

- **The control:** a `Depart`/`Arrive by` toggle button + a styled "Now" chip fronting a visually-hidden native `datetime-local` (`index.html:40-45`, chip logic `js/search.js:26-52`). No time is required; untouched means "now". The chip opens the native picker (`showPicker()`, `search.js:46`).
- **Flow into routing:** on search, a non-empty picker value is converted from Rome wall-clock to an ISO instant (`romeWallToIso`, `js/time.js:70-88`) and sent as `time` (+ `arriveBy`) (`search.js:309-314`). The proxy passes them through and pins `searchWindow=21600` (6 h), `numItineraries=6` (`server/proxy.js:543-557`). **Empty time = MOTIS defaults to now.**
- **Result scope:** one shot, up to 6 itineraries inside a 6 h window after (or before, for arriveBy) the chosen instant. **There is no later/earlier affordance anywhere in `js/`** — a user who wants the 17:00 departure after a morning search must guess a time into the picker.
- **Flow into the degraded path:** the racing direct lookup extracts `date` and `afterMin` from the picker value (`search.js:324-331`); `/api/direct` runs the *whole chosen day* (+ next day) against its own calendar logic (`server/proxy.js:616-649`, `serviceRuns` `:56-79`) — so ironically the fallback already has better time semantics than the primary path.
- **Defect:** with a value set, the clear button's expanded hitbox covers the entire when-row, so tapping `Depart`/`Arrive by` (or the chip) wipes the date silently (R-02, §7). The toggle also does nothing visible until the next search — no feedback that "Arrive by" now applies.

### 5.2 Target design — the control

**Recommendation: Google's tri-state semantics in Transit's now-weighted presentation** `[observed both — §3 A3]`. A during-the-trip app should keep "now" one tap away and the picker out of the fast path — but Sicily's sparse schedules make *arrive by* genuinely load-bearing (the 11:15 coach is the *only* way to make a 12:00 appointment in Agrigento), so it stays first-class, unlike Transit/Citymapper's buried version (Citymapper's own critics flag the buried time control `[reported]` Pratt 2026).

- **Collapsed (default):** one chip in the when-row: `Today · now ▾`. No separate toggle button (the Depart/Arrive toggle as a sibling control is retired — see Cut list, C-3; this also deletes the R-02 trap layout).
- **Expanded (bottom sheet, reusing `openSheet`):** three radio rows — `Leave now` / `Depart at…` / `Arrive by…` — where the two "at" rows inline the native `datetime-local` when selected, plus quick chips `Today` `Tomorrow` `Sat`. One `Apply` button; the sheet's title states the timezone ("Times are Italy time").
- **Collapsed states after apply:** `Today · now`, `Tue 28 · dep 15:00`, `Tue 28 · arr by 12:00`. The ✕ reset stays, but inside the sheet, not floating on the row.
- 390 px: chip is full-row width minus nothing else — the whole when-row is one target. No hitbox overlays possible.

### 5.3 The unspecified-time default: whole day, from now

**Recommendation: an untouched search covers *from now to the end of the service day*** (not the full day including departed runs). Reasons: (a) on the ground, a departed coach is pure noise — the brief's own framing; (b) the degraded `/api/direct` already behaves exactly this way (`afterMin` from now, `proxy.js:99`), so the two paths converge; (c) "what already left today" remains reachable via the stop-schedule sheets (Saved tab), which show past rows greyed (`js/saved.js:96-102`, `.sched-past`). The counter-case — a planner wanting the full-day shape ("is this corridor hourly or 3-a-day?") — is better served by the §5.7 grouping making sparseness visible than by listing dead departures. **Flagged as Open Question #1** since the brief asks; the from-now recommendation stands.

For an explicit **Depart at** time: window = chosen instant → end of that service day. For **Arrive by**: window = start of service day → chosen instant, rendered latest-first-nearest-the-deadline (MOTIS handles this natively: arriveBy searchWindow counts *departure* time bounds `[observed]` openapi).

### 5.4 Feasibility against the routing layer — verified, not assumed

Probed live against `api.transitous.org/api/v3/plan` (2026-07-27, transcripts §11) `[observed]`:

| Probe | Result |
|---|---|
| `searchWindow` semantics | Seconds; default **900 s = 15 min** (the F-1 trap, reconfirmed). Accepts 64800 s (18 h) without complaint. |
| Whole-day PA→Cefalù (18 h window) | **19 itineraries, 2.3 s, 417 KB raw** |
| Whole-day PA→Agrigento | 14 itineraries, 2.2 s, 551 KB raw |
| Same query through the prod proxy (slimming + gzip) | **74.5 KB slimmed → 4.6 KB gzipped, 4.0 s cold; 60 s proxy cache makes repeats instant** (`server/proxy.js:568`) |
| `numItineraries` | Is a *minimum*, only meaningful with `timetableView=true` (default); `maxItineraries` caps. |
| `pageCursor` | Works: response carries `nextPageCursor`/`previousPageCursor` ("keep the original request as is"); cursor `LATER\|1785240600` **must be URL-encoded** (raw pipe = HTTP 400 `[observed]`). Page fetch ~1.9 s. |
| `maxMatchingDistance=600` | Load-bearing: identical probes without it return **zero** itineraries (station coords don't attach). Any new query path must carry the full F-1 param set. |

**Recommendation: single whole-day call, not pagination-first.** `searchWindow = seconds from anchor to 03:00 next day`, `maxItineraries=24`, existing tuning params. Cost over today's query: ~+0.4–2 s upstream and ~3 KB more on the wire gzipped — negligible against the p50 1.5 s the audit already measured. Keep `pageCursor` for the *edges*: an `Earlier ▲` pill above the list (previousPageCursor) and `Later ▼` below for crossing into the next service day — the Transit/Citymapper paging pattern (A4) at the boundaries only. If Transitous latency degrades post-#2327 (more feeds, bigger graph), the fallback is the same UI backed by 6 h pages — the presentation (§5.7) is designed to not care which backend strategy feeds it.

Proxy change implied (implementation session, not this review): accept the client's `searchWindow` (already passed through, `proxy.js:554`) and a `maxItineraries` pass-through; cache key already includes both (`:559`).

### 5.5 The degraded path — same semantics

`/api/direct` already honors date + afterMin and spans today+tomorrow (`proxy.js:629-648`). Alignment needed: (a) when the plan path goes whole-day, the direct block should stop truncating at 10 results (`directSearch` slice, `proxy.js:118`) for the day view — return the day, let the client group; (b) `coachBoard`'s "all" sheet is today-only by design (`:233`) — fine; (c) **calendar correctness across a full day is already gated**: `serviceRuns` handles feriale / escluso-sabato (`mon-fri`) / festivo / scolastico / seasonal windows (`proxy.js:56-79`), mirrored from the pipeline's explicit `calendar_dates` math (`pipeline/emit_gtfs.py:68-98`), and `validate.py:23-47` asserts the named-date semantics (Ferragosto not in feriale, Easter Monday not in mon-fri, school service dark in Christmas break) on every build — I verified the assertions read correctly against the emit logic. The unit suite covers the same surface (`tests/unit/direct-fallback.test.js`).
One edge found and **confirmed live in the data**: `fmtMin`/dep formatting wraps hours `%24` (`proxy.js:106,146`), and `coach-trips.json` contains stop times up to **3170 min = 52:50** `[observed]` — such a leg renders as "04:50" with the day tag coming only from the day *loop*, so a past-midnight arrival can display as today. The whole-day view must derive the `+1`/`+2` tag from `minutes ≥ 1440`, not from `dayOff` alone (R-25). *(Corrected in §6.6 after direct measurement: the 52:50 outlier is not a long SAIS Trasporti run but two `GIORNALIERA - Lido Bellia` trips with stop times `0, 720, 3170` — parse damage that no current gate catches, R-16. The genuine past-midnight population is 104 SAIS Autolinee overnight runs, which R-25 must render correctly.)*

### 5.6 Service-day boundaries

Define **service day = 03:00–03:00 Rome** (no Sicilian coach in the feed departs 00:00–04:00; earliest observed 04:30). Late-evening searches: the whole-day window runs to 03:00 so post-midnight arrivals stay in view with the existing `+1` dayTag (`search.js:462-467` `[observed working]`); the `Later ▼` pill pages into tomorrow, whose runs render under a sticky "Tomorrow, Wed 29 Jul" header rather than interleaved. Trips whose service day differs from the calendar day (a 23:50 Saturday departure arriving Sunday) already carry Saturday's calendar in both MOTIS and `serviceRuns` — only the *label* needs care, and dayTag covers it.

### 5.7 Results presentation for a whole day

Pulled from A4/A9/C1/B4:

- **Grouping:** hour-of-day is too granular for 5-a-day corridors; group by daypart — `Morning` (to 12:00), `Afternoon` (to 17:00), `Evening` — as small sticky headers (`position:sticky` under the app header). A corridor with 19 departures reads as three scannable clusters; a 3-a-day corridor shows its sparseness honestly (one row per cluster, and that *is* the information).
- **The "now" line:** for from-now searches nothing is above the fold but the next feasible departure — no marker needed. For a **Depart at** search on today, a thin `— now —` rule sits between past-the-chosen-time and later groups if the user scrolls up via `Earlier ▲`.
- **Near-identical departures:** *do not auto-collapse.* On sparse data, two 07:00 coaches on different operators are different products (different terminals, tickets); Rome2Rio's prose-frequency trick (B4) is for low-confidence data, not for hiding real rows. The exception already exists: `/api/direct` dedups chain permutations (`proxy.js:203-211`).
- **Return to now:** after scrolling, a floating `↑ Next departure` pill (bottom-right, above the nav) scrolls back to the first future row — the map tab's locate-button pattern reused.
- **Row craft:** keep the current card anatomy (clock pair dominant); the C1/C2 discipline applies — the whole-day list is where 12 font sizes would turn into soup, so the type-scale pass (R-10) lands before or with this feature.
- **Cost display:** none in the row beyond what §4 defines — fares live in the detail sheet; the *card* gets at most the B1 badge once fares exist ("cheapest").

### 5.8 The setting

`Settings → Search results: Whole day (default) / Next departures only`, persisted via the storage adapter (`js/store.js:19-24` `patchSettings`) as `settings.resultSpan`. "Next departures" reproduces today's 6-itinerary behavior for users who find the day view long. **Default for a first-time user: whole day** — it is the honest representation of a sparse network, and the setting exists precisely so the minority who prefer a short list can shorten it. Lives in Settings under the theme row; no per-search toggle (the when-sheet is already the per-search control surface).

### 6.2 The search input under stress

**The when-row is broken in a way no amount of care at the keyboard avoids (R-02).** With a date set, tapping the `Depart` toggle reverts the chip to `Now` and empties the input — measured, not inferred: `chip=Now | toggle=Depart | input value=""` `[observed]` `phase4a-log.txt` A1. The mechanism is a three-rule collision, all three individually reasonable:

- `.field-clear` is `position:absolute; z-index:2` (`css/styles.css:344-348`) — correct for the From/To fields, which sit inside `.endpoint-row{position:relative}` (`:44`).
- QA-06b gave every clear button an invisible 6 px touch-target halo: `.field-clear::after{content:'';position:absolute;inset:-6px}` (`:502`). Inside a positioned parent this is a 42 px halo. 
- LH-08 made the *when*-row's clear button `position:static` so it flows in the flex row (`:541`).

A static parent is not a containing block, so that `::after` resolves against the next positioned ancestor — `.when-row{position:relative}` (`:546`) — and `inset:-6px` stretches it over **the entire row plus 6 px of bleed**, painted above the two in-flow buttons beneath it. Every tap on `Depart`, `Arrive by`, or the chip itself is a tap on the reset button. The one-line fix is `position:relative` on `.when-clear` (same flow behaviour, restores the containing block); §5.2's redesign deletes the layout entirely, but the fix should not wait for it.

**"Arrive by" does nothing on its own (R-01).** `departMode` only reaches the request inside `if (whenVal)` (`js/search.js:311-314`); with no time picked, tapping `Arrive by` changes a button label and nothing else — the search still departs now. The toggle also never sets `aria-pressed` (`toggleWhen`, `:271-274`), so assistive tech sees an unlabelled state change too. Combined with R-02 this is a trap with two jaws: setting a time arms the toggle, and touching the toggle disarms the time.

**Autocomplete dies silently (R-05).** With `/api/geocode` failing, `suggest()` catches and hides the list (`js/search.js:258-260`) — no toast, no inline hint, identical to "no matches" `[observed]` `b1-geocode-dead.png`. The PRD's honesty rule is explicit that absence must be explained; this is the one surface where it is not. A rural user on a dying connection sees only their own typing.

**Suggestions leak pipeline damage (R-12).** Typing "Marchesa" offers `per C.da Marchesa) Sciacca (deviazione per C.da Marchesa)` `[observed]` A6 — a fused fragment presented as a place a person could travel to. 31 stop names in the feed have unbalanced parentheses (R-13), including `AVOLA (P.zza Vitt. Veneto/P.zza Trieste))` and `.F.S.) PIRAINO(Staz. .F.S.)` `[observed, measured]`.

**Passing:** no horizontal overflow at 320 px, 390 px, 768 px, or 844×390 landscape (`overflow px=0` at all four) `[observed]` A8 — the QA-13 narrow-viewport work holds. Rapid double-submit produced one coherent result set (the `searchSeq` guard returns before `pushRecent`, so recents stay clean) `[observed]` A4. ### 6.3 Results and the degraded ladder

The three-rung ladder works and is the app's best feature. **The ladder has one rung it cannot climb (R-04).** Under a real 429 storm — 120 requests, 66 rejected `[observed]` — the app says *"No route found — the routing service may be unreachable."* Two failures in one string:

1. **Wrong diagnosis.** `getJson` throws `HTTP ${res.status}` (`js/api.js:12`) but the catch in `runSearch` (`:354-365`) discriminates nothing, so "you are asking too fast" renders as "the upstream is down" with no retry guidance and no wait time. 2. **The lifeboat is chained to the ship.** `rateLimited(ip)` gates every path beginning `/api/` (`:772`), so the 90/min bucket that exists to be polite to *Transitous* also kills `/api/direct` — a lookup against a JSON file already in the proxy's memory, costing no upstream call at all. **Eight stops with no service should not be favouritable (R-14).** They reach autocomplete and the Saved tab, where they are guaranteed to answer "No departures". `scolastico` and `0` are not places. The emit-time orphan filter that removed 651 quarantine-residue stops in v0.7.0 evidently runs before these are created, or against a different key.

**The validator has a blind spot (R-16).** `GIORNALIERA - Lido Bellia` carries stop times `0, 720, 3170` — 00:00, 12:00, then **52:50**, a 2-day 4-hour "day trip" to a beach. It survives because `validate.py`'s two-sided speed gate tests implied *speed*, and an absurdly long duration makes speed absurdly *low* — landing in the warning tier that legitimately holds slow school circuits. A span gate is the missing assertion: no coach trip should exceed ~12 h (only 6 do; 3 exceed 24 h). This is a small fix with an outsized payoff, because of R-25.

**R-25 — past midnight is currently unrepresentable.** `fmtMin` computes hours modulo 24 (`server/proxy.js:146`), so a stop time of 1490 min renders `00:50` with no day carried. The 104 genuine overnight SAIS runs are therefore already displayed ambiguously, and §5's whole-day view — which lists a full service day at once — turns that ambiguity into visible nonsense (a 23:40 departure "arriving" 00:50 above a 06:00 departure). The day tag must be derived from `minutes >= 1440`, not from the day loop. Fixing R-16 first matters: with the 3170-minute trip still in the feed, a correct day-derivation would proudly render `+2`.

### 6.7 Platform — Capacitor and the offline question

The wrap is a server-URL shell (`capacitor.config.json`), so the APK being one version behind the web app is a non-issue; it is the web app. The consequence worth stating: **the Android build gets no offline capability the PWA lacks**, and the PWA's offline capability is shell-plus-cache only — `/api/direct` is a server call, so the coach feed, the one dataset genuinely ours and genuinely static, is exactly the thing unavailable offline. Shipping `coach-*.json` (a few MB) into the app bundle and running the direct search client-side would make the app's differentiator work with no signal at all, which is the Sicilian rural case. That is a real project, not a tweak — Open Question #7.

### 6.8 Type and accessibility

The A7 census on a live results screen: **12 distinct sizes across 220 text nodes, 3 weights** — `16px×75, 14px×52, 12px×23, 20px×18, 16.8px×13, 11.52px×10, 19.2px×10, 13.6px×7, 11.2px×6, 12.8px×4, 15.2px×1, 12.48px×1` `[observed]`. Kayak's denser results page, measured the same way: **6 sizes, 2 weights, 657 nodes** `[observed]` `tierc-log.txt`. The fractional sizes are em-compounding — `.muted{font-size:0.875rem}` (`css:16`) nested inside `.suggest-area{font-size:0.75rem}` and friends. QA-09 fixed this once at the `.muted em` site; it has regressed through new nesting rather than through the old rule failing (R-10). A 6-step rem scale, applied where the compounding happens, is the fix — and §5.7's whole-day list is the deadline, because a long list is where 12 sizes stop being a nitpick.

Two smaller ones: `feedHorizonDate()` memoises its promise for the session (`js/search.js:373-376`), so a horizon that changes under a long-lived tab is never re-read (R-18); and `suggest()` clears the list twice in consecutive statements (`:204-205`), harmless dead code (R-21).

Four ships, ordered so each one clears ground for the next. Nothing here requires a decision from §10 except where marked.

## 9. Cut list

Things this review recommends *removing*, not adding. Each was reasonable when built.

- **C-1 — the second horizon sentence.** Not just deduplicated (R-03): the dead-end should state the horizon once, in the position where it is actionable, and `maybeHorizonNote` should own it exclusively.
- **C-2 — `numItineraries=6` as a product decision.** It is a MOTIS *minimum* that has been read as a cap since M0 (§5.4). Once the whole-day window ships, the "6" has no meaning worth preserving.
- **C-3 — the `Depart` / `Arrive by` toggle as a sibling control.** Retired into the when-sheet's three radio rows (§5.2). It is a mode switch with no visible state, it is inert half the time (R-01), and it is one of the two buttons the R-02 halo swallows. Removing it deletes the trap layout rather than patching it.
- **C-4 — `.field-clear::after` as a global rule.** The 6 px halo is correct only inside a positioned parent. Scope it to `.endpoint-row .field-clear::after` so it cannot escape again on the next control that reuses the class.
- **C-5 — the "Blank = leave now · times are Italy time" helper line.** Once the when-chip states `Today · now` and the sheet states the timezone (§5.2), this line is restating the control directly above it. It is also one of the `.muted` nodes feeding R-10.

## 10. Open questions

Eight, ordered by what they block. #1 and #3 gate Ships 3 and 4 respectively; the rest can wait.

1. **Whole day, or whole day from now?** §5.3 recommends *from now to end of service day* — a departed coach is noise, and it converges the plan path with `/api/direct`'s existing behaviour. The counter-case is the planner asking "is this corridor hourly or three-a-day?", which §5.7's daypart grouping arguably answers better than dead rows would. **Recommendation: from now, with `Earlier ▲` paging into the past-today edge.** Blocks Ship 3.
2. **Does "Next departures only" (§5.8) earn its place in Settings?** It exists to soften a long list for users who dislike it. If the daypart grouping works, nobody reaches for it, and it is a permanent second code path through the results renderer.
3. **Is a two-band range acceptable for AST?** AST is band-priced by road km; we can only sum haversine along the stop chain, which straddles at most two adjacent bands (§4.3) — displayed as `€4.10–5.00 · estimated`. The alternative is showing nothing for a large operator. **Recommendation: ship the range, labelled.** Blocks the AST portion of Ship 4.
4. **How stale is too stale for a fare?** §4.3 proposes 12 months → `stale` state. Sicilian coach tariffs move less often than that; a shorter window means more struck-through prices for no gain.
5. **Do the 14 coordinate pileups get fixed, or fenced?** Fixing means real geocodes for 14 clusters (pipeline work, uncertain yield). Fencing means the attach logic treats a pileup as one virtual stop and stops pretending they are distinct. Fencing is cheap and honest; fixing is better and slower.
6. **Should zero-trip stops be dropped from the feed or just hidden from autocomplete?** (R-14.) Dropping is cleaner; hiding preserves the diagnostic that something upstream produced a stop named `scolastico`.
7. **Client-side coach search for true offline (§6.7)?** Ships the feed into the bundle so the differentiator survives no-signal. Real project; the strongest argument for the Capacitor build existing at all.
8. **A manual strike (sciopero) banner?** Settings already warns that strikes never appear in schedule data. §3's A8 pattern — alerts that change *results*, not decorate them — is unreachable without a feed, but a hand-set banner is a few lines and Sicilian strikes are frequent and pre-announced. The cost is a maintenance obligation with no automation behind it.
